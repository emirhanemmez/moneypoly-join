Privacy Policy

We believe privacy should be simple and transparent. Here's everything we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Effective: January 1, 2025 Last updated: March 2025

1. Overview

Moneypoly ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application available on iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

By using Moneypoly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect the following information from your Google account:

  • Display Name — your full name as set in your Google account
  • User ID (UID) — a unique identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication
  • Email Address — used for account identification
  • Profile Photo — your Google profile picture, if available

We do not collect your password, payment details, location, contacts, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to provide and improve the app experience:

  • Creating and managing your in-app account
  • Displaying your name and avatar within the app
  • Saving your game progress and coin balance
  • Restoring your purchases across devices

4. Third-Party Services

Moneypoly uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage. Privacy Policy ↗
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management. Your User ID is shared with RevenueCat to manage purchase history. Privacy Policy ↗

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any other third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase servers. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Moneypoly is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data

To delete your account: open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Alternatively, contact us via email and we will process your request within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
